Introduction · Topic & Context
A technology riding the highest wave of expectation — while the evidence tells a quieter story.
What is agentic AI?
Systems that perceive, decide and act autonomously to pursue evolving goals — moving from passive assistants to coordinated multi-agent workers with memory and tool use.
The promise

Agentic enterprise value forecast by 2028
The reality

Why this matters now
01
Hype is driving real decisions
Klarna and DeepL cut ~1,000 jobs on AI promises — Klarna reversed after quality collapsed. Gartner: >40% of agentic projects cancelled by 2027.
02
Europe is under-studied
Most evidence is US-centred. EU data-sovereignty rules, adoption lag (4% vs 7%) and the AI Act create a distinct context.
03
The law predates the technology
The EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689) was drafted before autonomous agents existed — Arts. 12–14, 26 strain against multi-agent systems.

Hypothesis: P3 is plausible.
The controls the EU AI Act demands (Arts. 12–14) structurally overlap with the mitigations that make agents reliable. In Europe, building agentic AI for compliance and building it for value are the same engineering problem.
Reflections & Conclusion
The gap is real — but it's a system problem, not a model problem.
What the answer means
Driven by TCO under-estimation and organisational-readiness deficits — not model capability, and not EU regulation.
Fits the productivity J-curve: delayed benefits while complementary intangibles are built (EU 4% vs US 7% production use).
Compliance controls and reliability mechanisms converge (P3, supported by TRiSM literature).
Limitations
Thin Tier-1 cost evidence — 6 of 9 TCO components rest on Tier-4 triangulation; the gap is itself a finding. The N=86 survey measures perception, not impact — not causal proof.
Policy & practice
Budget against true TCO
Inference, orchestration, oversight, audit, retraining + EU sovereign-infrastructure premium — not vendor ROI decks.
Mandate Human + AI design
Human-in-the-loop as default: agents absorb repetition, humans keep judgment and strategic oversight.
Deploy from strategy, not hype
Start with high-value, low-complexity workflows; scale on evidence, not projection.
Recommendation — from 2026 research
Make P3 measurable. 2026 research shifted from single-shot accuracy to trajectory-level, tool-aware metrics — TRiSM's Component Synergy Score & Tool Utilization Efficacy (Raza et al., 2026). Adopt them as compliance-linked reliability KPIs to test P3 empirically.
