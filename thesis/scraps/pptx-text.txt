4 · REFLECTIONS
What I&apos;d flag, what to caveat, and what 2026 now confirms
Corrections &amp; honesty
One sub-scale — Organisational Context (α = .55) — is two-dimensional (policy vs. support); treated as formative and read with caution, not as a clean reflective scale.
HTMT discriminant values run high: constructs correlate. Findings are read as patterns, not causal claims.
AE5 (deskilling) reported standalone, not folded into augmentation.
Limitations
Cross-sectional &amp; self-reported — associations, not causation; perceived productivity, not audited output.
Purposive, non-probability sample (N = 86): analytic, not statistical, generalisation.
Next step: a longitudinal panel (12 &amp; 24 months) within one sector to test causal direction.
Latest research aligns
NBER WP 34984 (Baslandze et al., 2026): ~750 executives document the SAME productivity paradox — perceived gains exceed measured gains.
PwC AI Jobs Barometer 2026: a two-track market — AI-exposed junior roles now demand senior-level skills, echoing my pipeline concern.
Thomson Reuters 2026: field enters the “strategic phase” — governance &amp; workflow redesign, exactly the dividing line found here.
Bottom line:  
enhancement with a new oversight layer — not replacement; the productivity gain is perceived ahead of realised; and governance, not tool access, is what separates firms where people grow from firms where skills erode.
