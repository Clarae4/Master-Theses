4 · Reflections

What I’d flag, and what to caveat

Corrections & honesty
—

Organisational Context (α = .55) is two-dimensional (policy vs. support); treated as formative and read with caution.

—

HTMT discriminant values run high: constructs correlate. Findings read as patterns, not causal claims.

—

AE5 (deskilling) reported standalone, not folded into augmentation.

Limitations & next step
—

Cross-sectional & self-reported — associations, not causation; perceived productivity, not audited output.

—

Purposive, non-probability sample (N = 86): analytic, not statistical, generalisation.

Next step

A longitudinal panel (12 & 24 months) within one sector to test causal direction.
